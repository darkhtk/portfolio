[ 02 · RITUAL ]shuffle / cut / choose
// three-way cut

The three piles restack from the chosen pile.

After one pile is chosen, the visible piles recombine in that order. Reusing the cards already on screen keeps the transition from shuffle to selection continuous.

// draggable card wheel

All 78 cards are picked from a fan-shaped wheel.

Users turn the wheel with a finger and tap the card itself; the chosen card lifts out into a selection slot. The wheel keeps the same gesture across one-, three-, and seven-card readings.

[ 03 · PROBLEMS ]problems i solved
01

Separate animation from state

I keep shuffle, cut, wheel selection, and reveal as separate states. Moving cards are copies of the source cards, and a watchdog removes any layer left behind by an interrupted animation.

02

Move sharing work earlier

Generating a poster only after the share tap can outlive the mobile gesture window. A size-limited Blob is prepared as soon as the result is ready, so sharing starts immediately.

03

Handle iOS audio policy

The first user gesture unlocks the AudioContext, while per-action playback and duplicate suppression are managed independently. Shuffle and reveal feedback remains reliable on iOS.

// save · reopen · share

A saved reading can be reopened, deleted, or shared.

I store readings in the browser, render a Canvas poster, and pass it to Web Share. The Node server validates requests, limits request rates, caches static files, and exposes a health endpoint behind Docker and a NAS reverse proxy.

[ 03 · PROBLEMS ]problems i solved
01

Separate animation from state

Shuffle, cut, selection, and reveal are explicit stages. Source cards and moving instances have separate lifecycles, and a watchdog clears residual layers when motion fails to complete.

02

Move sharing work earlier

Generating a poster only after the share tap can outlive the mobile gesture window. A size-limited Blob is prepared as soon as the result is ready, so sharing starts immediately.

03

Handle iOS audio policy

The first user gesture unlocks the AudioContext, while per-action playback and duplicate suppression are managed independently. Shuffle and reveal feedback remains reliable on iOS.

// complete reading cycle

Users can save, reopen, delete, and share a completed reading.

The app stores readings in the browser, reopens or deletes them, generates a Canvas poster, and shares it through Web Share. The Node server handles validation, rate limiting, static caching, and a health endpoint behind Docker and a NAS reverse proxy.

// hardware

Where software alone
can't deliver the training.

Shaking a scaffold inside VR and making fall risk register required a platform that actually moves. The dual-axis scaffold rig is hardware we designed and built in-house to meet that requirement.

It then shipped alongside the simulators to Samsung, LG, and POSCO safety experience centers. The delivery-unit way of working — owning software, hardware, and operations as one package — was established here.

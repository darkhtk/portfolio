# Cover Letter (English)

Dear Hiring Team,

I am a senior Unity/XR systems engineer with 10 years of experience building real-time software across industrial XR, BIM runtime tooling, digital-twin authoring tools, robotics interfaces, and applied AI systems. The common thread in my work is practical ownership: I care about whether a system can survive real operators, delivery pressure, validation, maintenance, and repeated use in the field.

My Unity work has focused on systems that require deep runtime and tooling decisions, not just scene assembly. In **WatchBIM** and **Unity-CrossSection**, I worked on heavy-geometry interaction, cross-section generation, 2D analysis viewing, shader-based visualization, camera tooling, Burst/Job System processing, mesh caching, and reusable C# module structure for large BIM data. In **DXCenter**, I built a Unity 6 URP/WebGL authoring environment for industrial digital-twin content, applying ScriptableObject data flow, Service Locator, EventBus, Strategy-based state effects, and adapter-style device integration.

I have also worked on networked and robotics-facing Unity systems. In **VR Robot Teleoperation** and **MetaArm**, I connected Quest 3 input, Unity runtime logic, coordinate conversion, smoothing, Flask APIs, TCP communication, RoboDK, and robot safety constraints into usable XR control pipelines. These projects required C# depth, Unity engine-level understanding, real-time input handling, server integration, and careful treatment of reliability and operator safety.

More recently, I have been applying AI tools as an active development system rather than a simple code generator. **DeskRelay** is my clearest example: a self-host AI development control surface built through full-vibe coding with Claude Code. The project lets a browser or mobile surface operate Claude Code sessions running on local PCs, with server setup, registered connector daemons, LAN/Tailscale operation, WebSocket/SSE relays, bearer-token authorization, session queues, permissions, instructions, skills, update flow, diagnostics, and offline failure reporting.

What matters in DeskRelay is not that I delegated code to AI. It is that I used AI as a development organization: defining manager and worker roles, maintaining project instructions, challenging happy-path output, testing registration and recovery paths, refining product language, deciding what information should be hidden or shown, and repeatedly pulling surface bugs back into architecture, diagnostics, and operating flow. I use AI to amplify judgment, not to replace it.

I believe my strongest fit is with teams that need someone who can bridge implementation depth and delivery reality: Unity-based simulation, industrial XR, digital-twin software, robotics interfaces, geometry-heavy tooling, and AI-assisted development products where architecture and workflow matter as much as the model itself. I am most effective when I can take responsibility for both the technical core and the practical constraints around it.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would be glad to discuss how my experience could support your team and the specific problems you are solving.

Sincerely,
